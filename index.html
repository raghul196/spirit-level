What is a Spirit Level?

A spirit level (or bubble level) is a tool used to check if a surface is perfectly horizontal (level) or vertical (plumb). It's an essential tool for construction, carpentry, and home improvement.

A traditional spirit level has a small glass vial with a bubble inside. If the bubble is centered between the lines, the surface is level. This online spirit level uses your phone's sensors to do the same thing, but with digital precision.

A Note on Accuracy

While this online level is great for most DIY tasks, it's not a replacement for a professional, calibrated spirit level. The accuracy depends on your phone's sensors, and small bumps can affect the reading. For the best results, use the calibration techniques described below.

Understanding the Components

This online spirit level has two main modes: Surface and Wall. Here’s a quick guide to the components in each mode.

Surface Mode

  • Circular Dial: A bullseye level that shows the surface angle in all directions.
  • Main Bubble: The bubble in the circular dial. When it's in the center, your surface is level.
  • Vertical & Horizontal Levels: Two rectangular levels that show the pitch (front to back) and roll (left to right) angles.
  • Degree Display: Shows the exact angle of the surface in degrees.

Elements in Wall Mode

  • Wall Level Box: A classic spirit level interface for checking vertical surfaces.
  • Wall Bubble: The bubble in the wall level. Center it between the lines to find the perfect vertical angle.
  • Wall Degree: Shows the exact vertical angle in degrees.

How to level Surface using Spirit level online?

  1. Select 'Surface' Mode: Ensure the 'Surface' button is active in the mode slider.
  2. Place Device: Lay your smartphone or tablet flat on the surface you wish to measure.
  3. Observe Readings: The bubble and degree displays will show the tilt in real-time.
  4. Adjust Surface: Move or adjust the surface until the main bubble is centered within the circular dial, and both degree readings are 0.0°.

How to level a frame on wall?

  1. Select 'Wall' Mode: Ensure the 'Wall' button is active in the mode slider.
  2. Place Device & click Start: place your smartphone or tablet upright against the vertical surface you want to measure and press start.
  3. Observe Readings & Adjust Surface:The wall bubble and degree display will show the vertical tilt in real-time. Adjust the item or your device until the wall bubble is centered and the degree reading is 0.0°.

How to Improve Measurement Accuracy

You can significantly improve the accuracy of your measurement and compensate for sensor bias by using a simple two-step calibration method. This process effectively cancels out most of the phone's inherent zero-offset error.

  1. First Measurement: Place your phone on the surface you want to measure and note the angle reading (e.g. 0.8°).
  2. Second Measurement: Without lifting it, rotate your phone exactly 180° in the same spot and take a second reading (e.g. -1.0°).
  3. Calculate the True Offset: The true angle is the average of these two measurements. For example: (0.8 + (-1.0)) / 2 = -0.1°. This shows your surface is very close to level, with a small offset. A perfectly calibrated level would read 0.5° and -0.5° in this example if the surface had a 0.5° tilt.

Use the 180° rotation method to cancel out sensor errors and achieve a more accurate measurement.

Adjusting for Protruding Cameras

Modern smartphones often have camera bumps that prevent them from lying perfectly flat. This can interfere with accurate measurements, but you can easily correct for it using the Zero button, as illustrated below.

  1. Place and Observe: Position your phone on the surface. Notice that the camera bump creates a slight, stable tilt, showing a non-zero reading.
  2. Press Zero: Tap the Zero button. This instructs the spirit level to treat the phone's current tilted position as the new "0°" reference point.
  3. Measure Accurately: The display will now read 0°. The effect of the camera bump is cancelled out, allowing you to take accurate measurements relative to the surface.

Use "Zero" to compensate for the tilt caused by a camera bump.

What Can You Use a Spirit Level For?

An online spirit level is a handy tool for a variety of tasks:

  • Hanging Pictures: Make sure your photos and artwork are perfectly straight.
  • Assembling Furniture: Ensure tables, chairs, and other furniture are stable and level.
  • Leveling Appliances: Prevent washing machines from vibrating and ensure refrigerators run efficiently.
  • Construction and Carpentry: Check that walls are plumb and floors are level.
  • Photography: Avoid crooked shots by leveling your camera on a tripod.
