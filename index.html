What is a Spirit Level?

A spirit level, bubble level, or simply a level, is an instrument designed to indicate whether a surface is horizontal (level) or vertical (plumb). This online spirit level uses your device's motion sensors to replicate the functionality of a traditional level, providing a quick and easy way to check angles for your projects.

Uses

This tool is perfect for various household and professional tasks:

  • Hanging pictures and ensuring they are straight.
  • Installing shelves without a tilt.
  • Setting up a tripod for level photography.
  • Leveling a camper van or RV.
  • Checking the alignment of a TV mount.
  • DIY projects that require precise alignment.

FAQ

What is an online spirit level?

An online spirit level is a digital tool that uses your device's built-in sensors (like the accelerometer and gyroscope) to determine if a surface is level (horizontal) or plumb (vertical). It mimics the functionality of a traditional bubble level right in your web browser.

How do I use this tool?

For flat surfaces, select 'Surface Mode' and lay your phone flat. The bubble will indicate the angle of tilt. For vertical edges, like hanging a picture, select 'Wall Mode' and hold the side of your phone against the object.

Is this tool accurate?

The accuracy depends on the quality of the sensors in your device. For most modern smartphones, it is accurate enough for common household tasks like hanging frames, installing shelves, or leveling a table.
